Step 1 · Finalize

Is your deck and practice actually ready?

This checks the parts the software actually can verify: your deck passes its own quality checks, and you've completed your pacing practice.

    Step 2 · Prepare for Questions

    A short Q&A readiness drill

    Three questions, loosely matched to your deck's topic. Answer for real — each response is scored by keyword/rubric match, not free-text grading.

    Step 3 · The Real Thing

    Go deliver your presentation to a real audience

    Close this tab and actually present. A family member, a friend, a teacher, or a community group — anyone real. Use your deck, your practiced script, and what you just prepped in the Q&A drill. This is a genuine real-world task, not a simulation.
    🔍 Why this is a checkbox, not a "grade": this app has no way to observe or verify what actually happens during a real, offline, in-person presentation — no camera, no microphone left running, nothing sent anywhere. So this step is an honest self-report, not a fabricated score. Only check this once you've actually done it.
    Step 4 · Reflect

    Guided reflection

    Only you know how the real delivery actually went. This reflection is the pedagogically real part of this step — not a grade.
