Step 1 · Flat vs. Paced

The same words, delivered two different ways

Same script, same speaker. The only difference is pacing and pausing — but it changes everything about how it lands.

❌ Flat & rushed

No gaps, constant volume — everything runs together with no room to absorb a point.

✅ Paced & varied

Clear pauses (the flat stretches) after key points, with natural rises and falls in energy.

Pace, pausing, tone, projection — those four things separate delivery that lands from delivery that doesn't. You can't fix what you can't hear in your own voice, which is why you're about to record yourself.

Step 2 · Record Your Sample

Read this paragraph aloud, then listen back

About 60 seconds at a natural pace. Read it once, all the way through.

Your script

"Good afternoon, everyone. Thank you for taking the time to be here today. Over the next few minutes, I want to walk you through a simple idea that could change how our shop handles payments. Right now, every cash-only sale means a customer who might walk away if they don't have exact change. By accepting mobile payments, we remove that barrier completely — checkout gets faster, and we stop losing sales we never even notice we're losing. I'll show you exactly what this would look like, and what it would take to get started. Let's begin."
