The feedback loop

A teacher reads their work. Not an auto-grader.

Most free learning platforms mark answers right or wrong. Uttoron puts a person in the loop at the moments that matter.

Learn by doing

Students work through short interactive lessons — a live spreadsheet grid, a simulated PowerPoint ribbon, a typing trainer with finger-placement guidance.

Send real work

At set checkpoints they submit a typing result, a deck or a spoken recording to a volunteer teacher. Works offline: results are queued and sent when the connection returns.

Get tagged feedback

The teacher picks from a fixed vocabulary of tags and adds a short note. Students see it on their own Feedback Passport.

Fixed tags keep one volunteer fast and consistent

Each tag is either positive or constructive, so feedback stays specific and quick to give — even when one teacher reviews many students.

What students learn

Four tracks, one passport.

Finishing every lesson in a track earns a stamp in the student's Skills Passport. A passport is just a number and a 4-digit PIN — no email or phone needed, and it works from any device.

Typing

Typing fluency

Two grade bands, multi-session practice, WPM and accuracy scoring, and a baseline-versus-latest comparison so growth is visible.

Spreadsheets

Spreadsheet basics

Guided steps on a live grid — entering data, SUM, AVERAGE, MAX and MIN — each checked against what the student actually typed.

Data

Data accuracy & QA

Find errors, correct records, clean and validate data, then submit a quality report scored against an 80% pass mark.

Presentation

Presentation skills

Thirteen lessons practised on a simulated ribbon, then repeated in real PowerPoint, with three teacher-reviewed checkpoints.

Student privacy

Real student data stays off the public site.

✓

This page and the demo contain no real student information. Real accounts live on a separate production system behind sign-in.

✓

Passports use a number and hashed PIN — no email, phone number or address is collected.

✓

Submitted files are stored privately and shown only through short-lived links to the student and their reviewer.

✓

Built so each partner organisation's students are isolated from every other's at the database level.
