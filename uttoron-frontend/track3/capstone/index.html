Stage 1 · Inspect

Review the full batch and flag suspected errors

This is the largest batch you've worked yet — invoices, attendance sheets, and inventory logs together, some clean, some not. Flag anything you believe is wrong. Duplicates aren't part of this pass.

Stage 2 · Correct

Fix everything correctable, safely

Six records below have a verifiable issue you can fix directly. One can't be confirmed from the data on hand — leave it flagged for a supervisor. Undo is always available.

Stage 3 · Validate

Run the full validation pass

Run the rule engine across every record and scan the whole batch for duplicates. A failure on the record you flagged for a supervisor is expected — that's exactly why it's flagged.

Stage 4 · Submit

Score the day and submit the Quality Report

Your composite score weighs four things equally: how many true errors you caught, how many you fixed correctly, how often you flagged something that wasn't actually wrong, and how clean the batch is under independent rule validation. The Capstone stamp needs a composite score of 80% or higher — this isn't awarded for just clicking through.
