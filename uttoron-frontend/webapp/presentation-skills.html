Presentation Skills

Learn PowerPoint the hands-on way: practice each step on a built-in simulator, then do it for real in actual PowerPoint. Along the way, real reviewers give you feedback on what you build and how you deliver it — not just an auto-graded quiz.

🖱️

Practice on a simulator

Each lesson uses a simulated PowerPoint ribbon — click the real buttons, get instant feedback, no install needed.

🎯

Do it for real

Every lesson ends by sending you into actual PowerPoint (desktop or the free web version) to repeat the action for real.

🧑‍🏫

Real human feedback

Three checkpoints (Lessons 9, 12 & 13) send your work to a real reviewer — structure, design, pacing, and delivery.
